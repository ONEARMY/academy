## The Precious Plastic PRO Program 
We aim to have Precious Plastic workspaces financially sustainable. When workspaces are financially strong, they can create bigger impact, better products, and more local jobs. 
<br>

##  ❓ Why this program exists
At Precious Plastic, we empower local recycling workspaces to thrive. We know that with the right support, workspaces can grow their impact and tackle plastic waste more effectively.
But with limited resources and a growing global network, it’s become hard to support everyone in a meaningful way. We often struggle to identify which workspaces are truly active and committed over time, making it challenging to offer focused support. 
That’s where the PRO Membership comes in. 
It’s a new way for dedicated workspaces to stand out, gain exclusive benefits, and actively support the future of Precious Plastic. At its core, PRO is both a recognition of your contribution and a step toward building sustainable funding mechanisms that allows us to better support serious workspaces with the tools, visibility, and guidance they need.
<br>

##  ⭐ Why join?
Joining as a PRO Member helps you:
- Gain extra visibility on the platform to reach more customers and collaborators
- Benefits for more exclusive perks (coming soon)
- Support the global development of Precious Plastic tools and programs
- Be recognised as a committed and serious workspace in the community
<br>
The PRO Membership is designed to create a positive cycle:
- Workspaces join as PRO Members and support the community with a monthly membership
- This membership allows us to dedicate budget to improve resources, build better tools, and offer direct support
- As a result, PRO Members gain visibility, grow their skills and networks, and increase their recycling impact and income.
These perks will evolve and grow with the program.
More successful workspaces mean a stronger Precious Plastic community worldwide


## 📅 Where we are now
The PRO Membership is still in its early stages. 
As more workspaces join, the budget grows, allowing us to reinvest in better resources, exclusive perks, and practical support to help your workspace thrive and tackle plastic waste more effectively.

You can become PRO [here](https://preciousplastic.com/pro-membership)
